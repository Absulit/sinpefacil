Terms of Service

SINPE Fácil is an free and open source app, whose sole purpose is to provide information exchange between a Seller and a Client via QR or link that allows the generation of a SMS text message to be used with SINPE Móvil. No payments are processed, nor data is stored on servers, everything is encrypted locally on your device, this under our Privacy Policy. The app is offered as a technical convenience for community use and under the user's own supervision before sending a SMS. Its operation may depend on external factors (device, browser, telecommunication networks), therefore it is governed by the laws of the Republic of Costa Rica.
